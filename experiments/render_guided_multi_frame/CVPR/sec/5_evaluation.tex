\section{Experiments}

\subsection{Experimental Setup}

We evaluate MRigid4DGS on KITTI and KITTI-360. For localization, we report the root mean square error of the absolute trajectory error (ATE RMSE). For mapping, we report PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS.

\noindent\textbf{Implementation Details.}
We conduct our experiments on a machine equipped with NVIDIA GeForce RTX 3090 GPUs. We use RGB images, precomputed IGEV depth, stereo confidence, and precomputed sky masks as input. Feature correspondences are obtained using pyramidal Lucas--Kanade (LK) optical flow and validated with SuperPoint descriptor similarity. We use the same main configuration for KITTI and KITTI-360, sampling every second frame and optimizing Gaussian-to-object assignments over a sliding window of seven sampled frames with a step size of five. The render-guided assignment optimization uses 30 iterations with a learning rate of 0.08; its RGB and depth loss weights are set to 1.0 and 0.2, respectively. The rendering losses are evaluated at half resolution.

\noindent\textbf{Baseline Methods.}
For camera tracking, we compare with LOAM, ORB-SLAM3, MonoGS, SplaTAM, LSG-SLAM, 4DGS-SLAM, and Flow4DGS-SLAM. For scene reconstruction, we compare the rendering quality of MonoGS, SplaTAM, LSG-SLAM, 4DGS-SLAM, and Flow4DGS-SLAM.

\noindent\textbf{Metrics.}
For camera tracking, we report the root mean square error of the absolute trajectory error (ATE RMSE) in meters; lower values indicate more accurate trajectories. For scene reconstruction, we evaluate rendering quality using peak signal-to-noise ratio (PSNR), structural similarity index (SSIM), and learned perceptual image patch similarity (LPIPS). Higher PSNR and SSIM and lower LPIPS indicate better rendering quality.
